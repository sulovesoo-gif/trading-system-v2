## Multi-MA performance capital basis

Each strategy and observation receives a fixed KRW 10,000,000 baseline.  A leg
uses the maximum whole-share quantity affordable by its allocated ratio; unused
cash is not treated as invested.  The dashboard distinguishes realised profit
over actual closed-leg investment from realised profit over the fixed KRW
10,000,000 baseline.  A future multi-symbol daily total-return view uses total
realised profit divided by the sum of assigned symbol capital.
